Core Forestry Capabilities

Specialist Tree Planting Services

Delivering dedicated manual hand planting for commercial woodland owners, management companies, and private estates across the Central Belt and Galloway.

High-Volume Squad

Commercial Planting

High-tempo manual planting on prepared ground, including continuous mounding, inverted mounds, ploughed ridges, and scarified ground.

  • Sitka Spruce, Scots Pine, Larch & Douglas Fir
  • Strict adherence to target spacing (e.g. 2,500/ha)
  • Firm root-collar heel, zero J-rooting
Output: 12,000/day Type: Production
Clearfell Restock

Restocking Contracts

Replanting clearfell compartments with heavy brash mats, high stump density, and vegetation regrowth. Thorough manual screefing where required.

  • Heavy brash and slash navigation
  • Hylobius (pine weevil) defense awareness
  • Consistent spacing despite ground obstacles
Terrain: Heavy Brash Stock: Bare-root / Cell
Infilling

Beat-up (Infilling)

Surveying and infilling failed or weather-damaged compartments back to required stocking quotas. Fast walking pace across established brush.

  • Targeted infill of dead or damaged stock
  • Rapid ground coverage across wide compartments
  • Tube, spiral, cane and stake maintenance
Focus: Audit & Fill Pace: High Mobility
Native Schemes

Woodland Creation

Establishing native broadleaf and mixed woodlands under Scottish Forestry Grant Schemes (FGS) and Woodland Carbon Code compliance standards.

  • Oak, Birch, Rowan, Alder, Hazel & Scots Pine
  • Ecological micro-siting to soil moisture & aspect
  • Riparian buffer zones and deer fenced enclaves
Standard: FGS Grant Type: Mixed Native
